Communications Consent

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

By providing your contact information to Sell Simple CA through our website forms, phone calls, text messages, or other communications, you expressly consent to be contacted by Sell Simple CA and its representatives regarding your property inquiry or our real estate services.

How We May Contact You

With your consent, Sell Simple CA may contact you using the information you provide, including:

  • Phone calls to the number you provided
  • Text messages (SMS) to your mobile number
  • Emails to the address you provided
  • Follow-up communications regarding your property or inquiry

Text Message (SMS) Communications

If you provide a mobile phone number, you consent to receive text messages from Sell Simple CA related to your inquiry, property, or our services.

Message frequency may vary. You may receive multiple messages depending on the status of your inquiry and our communications.

according to your wireless carrier plan.

To opt out of text messages at any time, reply STOP. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further texts unless you re-opt in.

For assistance with text messages, reply HELP or contact us directly through our website or by phone.

Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Phone Call Communications

You consent to receive phone calls from Sell Simple CA at the number you provide. Calls may be made using manual or automated dialing systems. You understand that consent is not a condition of purchasing or selling property.

Email Communications

You consent to receive emails from Sell Simple CA at the email address you provide. You may unsubscribe from email communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.

Your Consent Is Voluntary

Providing your contact information and consenting to communications is voluntary. Consent to receive marketing or informational communications is not a condition of purchasing or selling property or obtaining our services.

Withdrawing Your Consent

You may withdraw your consent to communications at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any text message
  • Clicking unsubscribe in any email
  • Contacting us directly through our website or by phone at (916) 713-7718
  • Requesting removal by emailing offers@sellsimpleca.com

Record of Consent

When you submit a form on our website, we record the date, time, and IP address of your submission as evidence of your consent. This record is maintained in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA).

Changes to This Consent Notice

We may update this Communications Consent notice periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
